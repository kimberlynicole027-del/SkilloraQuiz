FEATURED COURSE

Basic Office Administration Skills

A skills-based course that introduces students to essential knowledge and practical skills needed in an office environment.

COURSE OUTLINE & LESSONS

I-click ang bawat lesson sa kaliwa para makita ang detalye nito sa kanan:

LESSON 1

Introduction to Office Administration

Students will learn the basic concepts, roles, responsibilities, and importance of office administration.

LESSON 2

Workplace Communication Skills

Students will develop proper communication skills for professional interactions in an office setting.

LESSON 3

Basic Computer & Office Software Skills

Students will learn basic computer functions and common office software used for everyday administrative tasks.

LESSON 4

Records and File Management

Students will learn how to properly organize, store, manage, and retrieve office records and files.

LESSON 5

Professionalism & Workplace Ethics

Students will develop professional behavior, responsibility, teamwork, and ethical practices in the workplace.
